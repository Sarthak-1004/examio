# Roles and permissions

## Implemented model and gaps

`users.isPlatformAdmin` controls platform administration. Membership roles are `school_admin`, `teacher`, `student`, `parent`, `finance`; status is active/revoked. Multiple roles/memberships are possible. The parent portal shows linked children (Phase 5); the finance role works in the Fees area (Phase 8).

Implemented capability layer (`packages/core/src/capabilities.ts`): school services call `withCapability`, which reads the caller's active roles from the database in the same transaction and denies by default. Current capabilities: `school.view` (all five roles), `school.setup.view` (all five roles), `school.files.upload` (school admin, teacher), `school.teaching.view` (teacher: own assignments), `school.roster.view` (teacher: students of sections taught today), `school.children.view` (parent: linked children), `school.own_record.view` (student: own record), `school.attendance.mark` (school admin, teacher), `school.timetable.view` (all five roles), `school.homework.manage` (school admin, teacher), `school.homework.view` (school admin, teacher, student, parent), `school.homework.submit` (student), `school.announcements.publish` (school admin, teacher), `school.announcements.view` (all five roles), `school.fees.view` and `school.fees.manage` (school admin, finance), `school.fees.pay` (parent), `school.questions.manage` and `school.practice.manage` (school admin, teacher), `school.practice.take` (student), `school.exams.manage` (school admin, teacher), `school.exams.take` (student), `school.results.grade` (school admin, teacher), `school.results.view` (student), `school.exams.publish`, `school.results.publish`, `school.admissions.manage` and `school.library.manage`, `school.inventory.manage` and `school.transport.manage` (school admin only), `school.library.view` and `school.transport.view` (student), and for school admins only `school.fees.approve`, `school.questions.publish`, `school.attendance.view`, `school.attendance.manage`, `school.timetable.manage`, `school.students.view`, `school.students.manage`, `school.guardians.manage`, `school.member_counts.view` (teachers lost it in Phase 4), `school.setup.manage`, `school.staff.view`, `school.staff.manage`, `school.members.view`, `school.accounts.recover`, `school.invitations.manage`, `school.support_access.view` and `school.audit.view`. Platform admins hold an explicit list: `school.view` and `school.members.view`.

A platform admin has platform access only after enrolling an authenticator app (two-step sign-in); this is enforced in the database. Platform admin scope: routine access covers schools, lookups, platform admins and school admins (onboarding and recovery). Any other school record requires an open support access grant for that school: at most 4 hours, with a reason, not extendable, written to the school's audit log and visible to its admins. The platform school list shows member totals as an aggregate only. A non-member or another school's identifier gets not-found; a member without the capability gets forbidden. Add a capability for every new service; do not test role names in services.

Current services support platform school/lookup management and self-service authentication. Services reject another school's identifier, and platform services require a signed-in platform admin that the database also recognises. Identity and membership writes are restricted in the database (task 01.1): no direct self-update, school admins manage only users exclusive to their school, cannot attach users of another school or platform admins, and cannot change membership roles or delete memberships. Since Phase 4 (migration `0011_staff`) a teacher reads only staff users and memberships of the school (admins, teachers, finance), their own staff profile and their own assignments; students and parents are no longer visible to teachers. Staff profiles and assignments are written by school admins only, assignments are ended rather than deleted, and a closed year's assignments are read-only. Database helpers `app_teaches_section(section, date)` and `app_teaches_subject(section, subject, date)` answer whether the caller teaches a section or subject on a date (assignment or class teacher, within its dates, while the staff profile is active); Phase 5 enrollment policies use them to show teachers their own students. Since Phase 5 (migration `0012_students`) a student record and its enrollments are readable by the school's admins, the student themself, a parent with an active guardian link, and a teacher who teaches the student's current section today (`app_teaches_student`; enrollments only while in force today); guardian links by admins and the guardian; student documents by admins only. Only admins write them. A parent gains a child only through a link the school creates (directly, or by a code naming the child that the parent accepts); there is no lookup by admission number or phone. Since Phase 6 (migration `0013_attendance_timetable`) attendance records are read by admins, by teachers of the section on that date, by the student and by linked parents; teachers write only their sections' registers, inside the school's edit window, never for future dates and never on or before the lock date; admins write any date (the service requires a reason for corrections and locked dates); a closed year is read-only. The timetable is readable by every member and written by admins. Since Phase 7 (migration `0015_homework_announcements`) homework is read by admins, its author, teachers of the section and, once published, the section's current students and their linked parents; submissions only by the student, their parents and reviewing staff, and written only by the student until feedback is returned; files attached to either are readable by exactly those readers; teachers publish notices only to sections they teach. Upload purposes carry their own capability (students may upload only `homework_submission` files). Since Phase 8 (migrations `0017_fees`, `0018_finance_reads`) fee data is read and written by school admins and finance staff; finance staff also read student records, enrollments and guardian links (billing minimum); linked parents read their children's invoices, payments and ledger and may only open a pending online payment; the ledger is append-only; concessions, adjustments and fee structures need a school admin's approval, and nobody decides their own request; online payments are captured only by the signature-verified provider webhook in the worker context. Since Phase 9 (migration `0019_question_bank`) chapters and topics are read by members and written by admins and teachers of the subject; questions, versions and question images are readable and writable only by admins and teachers currently assigned to the subject (students and parents have no access, so answer keys never reach learners); teachers cannot publish or archive; published versions are locked. Capabilities express roles; the database policies express the assignment, ownership and guardian-link scope. Do not expose new CRUD based on RLS alone.

## Target authorization rules

Deny by default. Authenticate, verify active account, unsuspended school, membership, capability, assignment/ownership and record state on every server operation. Permissions from active roles may combine within the selected school; another school's roles never apply. A global platform role is not an automatic routine entitlement to student records: audited support access must be designed before production broad support tooling.

Scope keys: **school** = selected tenant; **assigned** = current authorized class/subject assignment; **own** = authenticated user's records; **child** = verified active guardian link. State restrictions apply even when a capability is granted.

## Deletion and sensitive transitions

Draft records may be deleted only if unused and specifically authorized. Archive people/setup/questions instead of deleting referenced history. No hard deletion of attempts, released results, posted receipts or audits through ordinary UI. Revoke memberships/guardian links rather than erasing evidence. Personal-data erasure is a separate reviewed retention workflow.

Publication, promotion, suspension, fee reversal and post-release marks changes require server-side state validation and audit. Finance adjustments require distinct requester/approver identities when staffing permits; exceptions must be explicitly recorded and reviewed. Never allow users to promote themselves by editing membership roles, identity fields or tenant cookies.
